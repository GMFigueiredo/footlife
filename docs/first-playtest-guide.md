# First Playtest Guide

## Start the game

Open `index.html` in the project folder with a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox). The game runs entirely on your computer; it does not need a server or account.

To reset your run, use **New story** in the profile card on the desktop screen.

## What this first version includes

1. Character creation: player name, Brazilian birth region, and an initial football instinct.
2. Four condensed youth choices, from age 10 onward.
3. A senior start at age 16 for Estrela do Porto.
4. A full 14-round Liga Nacional Aurora calendar, with eight original clubs and evolving standings.
5. Three interactive match moments with shown success chances per fixture.
6. A personal-computer interface with Calendar, Messages, News, Career, Finance, Social, Photos, World Data, Leagues, and Records.
6. Local save data: refreshing the page preserves the current story.

## Suggested test run

1. Create a player and choose any region and starting instinct.
2. Read each youth scenario and choose the option that best suits the player you want to become.
3. On the desktop, open **Career** and confirm the relevant attributes changed.
4. Open **Messages** and **News** to see the youth story recorded.
5. Open **World Data**, **Leagues**, and **Records** before playing; these are the world information and history surfaces.
6. Open **Calendar** and choose **Go to match**.
7. In the match, compare the displayed chance values and make choices. Try a second story with a different starting instinct to see whether those values change.
8. At full time, open **News**, **Messages**, **Leagues**, and **Records**. Confirm that the match result, standings, income, and career history were remembered.
9. Play a second fixture. Confirm that the opponent and headline are different, and that the fixture round advances.
10. Refresh the browser page. Confirm your player and season remain available.

## Feedback to capture

- Which youth or match choice felt meaningful, and which felt unclear?
- Did the interface feel like a footballer's personal computer rather than generic software?
- Did the match provide enough tension and football context?
- Was there a moment when you expected the game to remember something but it did not?
- Did anything look broken, overlap, or fail to respond?

This is a deliberately small vertical slice. It validates the core feeling before animated pitch movement, broader club/world data, finance decisions, images, and the full scenario library are added.
